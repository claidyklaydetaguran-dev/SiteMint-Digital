Process

No mystery, no big reveal.

How a SiteMint project runs.

Five stages, each with a concrete output. You always know where the project is, what happens next, and what you can already use.

Plan My ProjectSee pricing

  1. You complete a short structured brief — what you do, what keeps falling through, what a win looks like. We read it properly before we ever get on a call, so the first conversation starts in the middle, not at the beginning.

    What you receive: The brief itself — the same one you'd fill in at Start — plus a one-page written recommendation.

  2. You see the system taking shape early — real pages, real flows, real words — not a surprise unveiling at the end. Feedback lands while it's still cheap to act on.

    What you receive: A reviewable staging link with real pages and real copy — comment or approve directly on it.

  3. We ship in focused stages you can use, connected to your calendar, phone, and records from the start.

    What you receive: A working staging environment, already wired to your calendar, phone number, and records.

  4. Every stage is verified before the next begins — typechecked, tested, and reviewed, with the standing rules below applied throughout.

    What you receive: A short verification note: what was tested, what passed, and what changed since the last stage.

  5. Every automated path launches with a human handoff and an audit trail. We watch the first weeks of real traffic together and tune what it teaches us. Systems drift as businesses grow — we stay available for the adjustments that keep yours matched to reality.

    What you receive: Your live system, plus a written tuning log from the first weeks of real traffic.

The standing rules

Four things that are true in every stage.

  • You can always see the state of the work. No status meetings required to find out what's happening.
  • Plain language, always. If we can't explain a decision simply, we haven't finished making it.
  • You can stop at any stage. Each checkpoint leaves you with something whole, not a construction site.
  • Your data and access stay yours. Credentials, content, and records belong to your business from day one.

Stage one takes about ten minutes.

The discovery brief is short, structured, and genuinely read.
