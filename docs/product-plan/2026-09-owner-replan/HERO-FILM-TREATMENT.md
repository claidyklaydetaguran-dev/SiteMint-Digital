## Homepage — "The system takes shape"

**Winning base frame:** over-shoulder engineer at a warm studio desk, dual monitors
with abstract unreadable layout blocks, morning sun + teal accent (candidate A).

**Ambient loop (produced):** slow push-in, engineer typing calmly, screen glow
shifts, dust motes, teal light breathing. Loop-friendly, no cuts.

**Narrative 15 s, 3 shots (maps the owner's 10 beats):**
1. **Brief → organization (beats 1–3):** the engineer reviews a printed client
   discovery brief beside the laptop, annotates it, pins brand colors and audience
   notes; AI-assist visualized as soft suggestions appearing as abstract cards on
   the monitor (unreadable).
2. **Inside the system (beats 4–5):** camera dives toward the screen and travels
   through an abstract connected-system space — a glowing Glacier-mint signal
   thread passes a form node, a webhook junction, a record card, a tracking pulse,
   an automation gate (all abstract geometry, no text).
3. **Resolved work (beats 6–10):** pull back out to the desk at golden hour — the
   engineer leans back with quiet satisfaction as the monitors show finished,
   polished compositions (soft-focus placeholders; REAL SiteMint interface captures
   are composited into these screen areas in post at page level); the scene's teal
   accent light hands off to the site's particle field (page-level transition).

**Composition on page:** film panel occupies ~55–60 % upper/right; live headline +
CTAs stay left; particle field remains visible lower/left; one mint signal thread
(SVG) travels between field and film. Mobile: poster + particles, no autoplay.

## AI Receptionist — "Answered"

**Winning base frame:** welcoming front desk, phone with a soft pulsing teal ring,
morning light, no people (candidate A). Human beat: owner-in-workshop (candidate B)
reserved for the page body or a future cut. Closing beat: dusk desk (candidate C).

**Ambient loop (produced):** the teal ring pulses like a call being answered,
curtains sway, nothing else moves. Loop-friendly.

**Narrative (deferred, within receptionist allocation if desired):** incoming call →
ring glow steadies (answered) → abstract rules/availability lookup as a mint thread
touching a calendar motif → a confirmation chime of light → cut to the REAL
dashboard outcome (composited capture). The interactive call theater stays above
the film, labelled simulated until live certification.
