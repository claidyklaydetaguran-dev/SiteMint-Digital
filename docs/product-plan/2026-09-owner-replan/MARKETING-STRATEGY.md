## The narrative (used everywhere, adapted per page)

- **Problem** — Growing businesses often have websites, forms, spreadsheets, messages,
  CRMs, and AI tools that do not work together.
- **Solution** — SiteMint designs the connected digital system — website, web app, CRM,
  automation, or AI experience — that moves information and work forward.
- **Outcome** — Fewer dropped inquiries, less repetitive administration, more organized
  operations, and clearer next actions.
- **Differentiation** — systems designed around the business; strategy and engineering
  together; AI used intentionally; connected workflows rather than isolated tools; human
  oversight retained; no exaggerated AI promises.

problem does it solve? What outcome does it create? What should the visitor do next?

## Copy rules

1. Headline hierarchy per hero: eyebrow (context) → headline (outcome, not feature) →
   supporting line (mechanism + honesty) → actions → visual.
2. Every section ends with a next step (link or CTA) — no dead-end sections.
3. Ban list (already contract-enforced on receptionist page, now site-wide guidance):
   "24/7", "never miss a call", "answers every call", "live now" before certification;
   invented numbers; "trusted by X businesses".
4. The word "Signal" stays out of public copy (owner decision, September replan).
5. AI Receptionist hero uses the owner-specified hierarchy verbatim:
   eyebrow "SiteMint AI Receptionist"; headline "Missed calls shouldn't mean missed
   opportunities."; support "Give callers a helpful next step—even when your team cannot
   answer—using your business information, availability, and appointment rules.";
   primary "Explore the Interactive Preview"; secondary "Request Beta Access";
   text link "Already a client? Sign in".
