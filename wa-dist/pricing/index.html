What affects the price

Six things that move a project between tiers.

The three tiers above are starting points, not the final word — these are the factors that shift a project up or down from its starting price, and every one of them gets discussed plainly during discovery, before we commit to a number.

Number of pages & flows

A five-page brochure site and a twenty-page site with gated content are different builds, even at the same tier.

Integrations & CRM connections

Connecting to a calendar or an existing tool takes less time than replacing that tool with a SiteMint CRM.

Automation complexity

A single follow-up sequence is a different scope than routing, reminders, and record-keeping across your whole follow-up flow.

Content & design readiness

Ready copy and brand assets move faster than starting from a blank page — we'll tell you which applies during discovery.

Timeline

A compressed launch date can change scope or cost; discovery is where we'll say so plainly, not after you've committed.

Ongoing support

Launch-only versus continued tuning and support changes the total, not just the build price.
