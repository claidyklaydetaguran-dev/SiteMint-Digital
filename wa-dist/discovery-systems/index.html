Discovery Systems

Turn first contact into a useful brief.

A discovery system asks the right questions in the right order, adapts to the answers, and hands your team something they can price, plan, and respond to — the same day.

Plan My Project
01 · The problem

"Contact us" forms produce mysteries, not opportunities.

Name, email, message. That's what most businesses know about a new opportunity — and so the first three exchanges are spent asking questions a form should have asked. Slow first responses lose deals, and the busier you get, the slower they get.

02 · What we build

Structured intake that respects the visitor and arms your team.

We design guided discovery flows that feel like a good first conversation, not a tax form — branching on what the visitor actually needs and producing a brief on the other end.

  • Guided multi-step flows with save-and-resume, not walls of fields
  • Branching logic — a booking inquiry and a support request take different paths
  • A structured brief delivered to your team, not a two-line email
  • Instant, honest acknowledgment to the visitor about what happens next

03 · How it works

From click to brief.

  1. 01

    Ask like an expert

    We script the questions your best salesperson would ask, in the order they'd ask them — and cut every question that doesn't change your response.

  2. 02

    Adapt to the answers

    The flow branches on need, urgency, and readiness, so nobody answers questions that don't apply to them.

  3. 03

    Produce the brief

    Answers land as a structured brief: what they need, what changes for them, what happens next.

  4. 04

    Route and follow up

    The brief reaches the right person with a follow-up already scheduled — connected to automation when you want it.

04 · Capabilities

What a discovery system can do.

Guided flows

Step-by-step intake with progress, save-and-resume, and mobile-first ergonomics.

Branching logic

Different needs take different paths — no irrelevant questions, no dead ends.

Structured briefs

Every submission arrives organized: need, scope, timing, urgency, context.

Instant routing

The right person is notified with the full brief attached, the moment it lands.

System of record

Submissions become records automatically — no copy-paste, no lost context.

Honest acknowledgment

Visitors are told exactly what happens next, and it actually happens.
