02 — How it connects

One connected system, from first visit to a customer helped.

A missed call, a form no one answers, a lead that never gets a follow-up. Scroll the diagram — or select a stage — to see where inquiries usually die, and how a connected system keeps them moving instead.

One thread, from first visit to customer.

Customer helped

The inquiry ends as a booking, a completed job, or a new customer — recorded where the whole team can see it.

Outcome: attention became a customer
What happens behind the scenes
  1. Website. Pages built around one clear next action, with visit-to-inquiry tracking wired in.
  2. Capture. Forms and call handling write structured inquiries — no retyping from an inbox.
  3. CRM record. Each inquiry becomes a customer record with contact details, interest, and priority.
  4. Workflow automation. Rules assign owners, schedule reminders, and send follow-up sequences that stop when a human replies.
  5. AI conversation. The AI receptionist answers from approved business information only, and hands off with a summary.
  6. Resolution. Bookings, tasks, and outcomes update the same record, so reporting reflects reality.

04 — CRM & Internal Systems

Where the business runs, in one place.

For teams whose leads live in a spreadsheet, an inbox, and someone's memory: the customer list, tasks, and follow-up the owner actually looks at — built around how the business already works instead of forcing a generic template onto it. The map below walks the same seven steps a real project moves through — every name in it is invented for illustration; the real Operations CRM is never shown publicly.

A customer-records connection is part of the Growth and Custom systems above, set up around how you work during discovery — it's the record every website form, automated follow-up, and receptionist call below writes to, so nothing lives in two places.
