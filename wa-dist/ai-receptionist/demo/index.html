01 · The dashboard tour

Five surfaces a business actually uses.

01

Overview

The workspace a business owner lands on: today's activity, recent call outcomes, and what needs a human next — one screen instead of a voicemail inbox.

02

Calls

Every handled call as an operational record: who called, what the receptionist did, the outcome, and what was handed to a person. No raw recordings are retained by default.

03

Contacts & follow-ups

Callers become contacts with history attached, so follow-ups happen from context — not from memory.

04

Appointments

Bookings the receptionist takes land on a real schedule, with confirmations tracked through to completion.

05

Availability & configuration

The business stays in charge: open hours, appointment types, and what the receptionist may offer are configured here — every automated behavior has an off switch.

02 · CRM & operations

Where the call ends up: a capability demonstration.

The map below walks the same seven steps a project or inquiry moves through in a SiteMint-built CRM. Every name in it is invented for illustration — the real SiteMint Operations CRM is private, role-protected, and never shown publicly.

  1. 01 · Workflow step

    Discovery submitted

    What comes in
    A structured intake brief — business details, goals, current tools, and budget range — submitted through the discovery flow, never a bare contact form.
    What SiteMint organizes
    SiteMint creates a new project record and a contact record the moment the brief lands, tagged with its source and every intake answer attached as notes.
    What automation may run
    The submission lands in the follow-up system automatically, and an acknowledgment email goes out within a minute. No automation touches pricing or scope.
    What the team sees
    A new card in the Discovery column with the full brief attached, visible to the assigned reviewer — nothing is client-facing yet.
    Useful next action
    A reviewer reads the brief and replies with a straight answer or books the discovery call.
